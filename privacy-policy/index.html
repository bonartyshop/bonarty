Privacy Policy for Bonarty Pin Scheduler

Last updated 30 September 2026

Bonarty Pin Scheduler is a private tool used only by the owner of the Bonarty Etsy shop. It creates and schedules Pinterest Pins for the shop's own listings of printable digital wall art.

What the app accesses

The app connects only to the shop owner's own Pinterest account, with the owner's permission, through Pinterest's official API. It uses that access to:

  • read and create boards on that account
  • create Pins from the shop's own listing photos, each linking to the matching Etsy listing
  • read basic account and Pin information needed to avoid posting duplicates

Who uses it

Only the shop owner. No other Pinterest users sign in to the app, and it does not act on anyone else's account.

What is stored

The access tokens needed to stay connected to the owner's Pinterest account, stored locally on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect, store, or process personal data about any other Pinterest user.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Pinterest account settings, after which the stored tokens stop working.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Bonarty shop's Etsy messages at bonarty.etsy.com.
